GEO World War 2 Models Mod - credits
====================================

The GEO tank models in Hearts of Oak (tanks, tank destroyers, assault guns, SPGs, SPAA and the
armoured cars used on tank chassis, with their textures and animations) are the work of
Kakheti Kartli, and are used here with the author's permission.

  Mod:     GEO World War 2 Models Mod - https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3132985068
  Discord: https://discord.gg/zJBQJt6GgA

Credit for these models belongs to Kakheti Kartli. Please don't take them out of Hearts of Oak
for your own mod; ask the author on the GEO Discord instead.

What comes from GEO
-------------------
  gfx/models/units/tanks/**, gfx/models/units/vehicles/**   GEO tank models, textures and animations
  gfx/entities/_geo_*                                       GEO's entity and mesh definitions for them
  gfx/entities/units_tanks.asset, dod_units_tanks.asset     GEO's versions of these files
  gfx/interface/equipmentdesigner/graphic_db/_geo_tank_*    GEO's tank model lists, without the icon lists
  gfx/interface/equipmentdesigner/graphic_db/zzz_oak_geo_models.txt
                                                            which GEO model each Oak chassis shows

Changes for Oak
---------------
  - Only the tank side of GEO is included. Infantry, small arms, planes, ships and helicopters
    were left out, and those keep the base game's look.
  - GEO's copies of paid-DLC unit files are not included, and entities built on paid-DLC
    models are left out, so players without those DLCs never get blank models.
  - GEO's icon lists come from Lamp's Eqpt Icons; Oak keeps its own equipment icons.
  - One stray closing brace in _geo_Japan_vehicles.asset was removed, and the base game's
    JAP_medium_armor_entity was put back into units_tanks.asset (GEO's version leaves it out).
